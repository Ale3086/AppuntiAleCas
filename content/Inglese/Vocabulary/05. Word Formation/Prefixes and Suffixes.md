Vocabulary for word formation. Crucial for language certifications (Use of English).

## 1. Negative Prefixes (Opposites)

<details>
<summary><b style="color: #569cd6;">Un-</b> (Usual ➔ <b>Unusual</b>) - <i>It is highly <b>unusual</b> for it to snow in May.</i></summary>
Insolito - <i>È altamente <b>insolito</b> che nevichi a maggio.</i>
</details>

<details>
<summary><b style="color: #569cd6;">Un-</b> (Happy ➔ <b>Unhappy</b>) - <i>He was very <b>unhappy</b> with his exam results.</i></summary>
Infelice - <i>Era molto <b>infelice</b> dei risultati del suo esame.</i>
</details>

<details>
<summary><b style="color: #569cd6;">In-</b> (Visible ➔ <b>Invisible</b>) - <i>The stealth airplane is almost <b>invisible</b> on the radar.</i></summary>
Invisibile - <i>L'aereo stealth è quasi <b>invisibile</b> sul radar.</i>
</details>

<details>
<summary><b style="color: #569cd6;">In-</b> (Formal ➔ <b>Informal</b>) - <i>You can wear jeans, it's a very <b>informal</b> party.</i></summary>
Informale - <i>Puoi indossare i jeans, è una festa molto <b>informale</b>.</i>
</details>

<details>
<summary><b style="color: #569cd6;">Im-</b> (Possible ➔ <b>Impossible</b>) - <i>It is mathematically <b>impossible</b> to divide by zero.</i></summary>
Impossibile - <i>È matematicamente <b>impossibile</b> dividere per zero.</i>
</details>

<details>
<summary><b style="color: #569cd6;">Ir-</b> (Regular ➔ <b>Irregular</b>) - <i>English is famous for its <b>irregular</b> verbs.</i></summary>
Irregolare - <i>L'inglese è famoso per i suoi verbi <b>irregolari</b>.</i>
</details>

<details>
<summary><b style="color: #569cd6;">Il-</b> (Legal ➔ <b>Illegal</b>) - <i>Downloading copyrighted movies for free is <b>illegal</b>.</i></summary>
Illegale - <i>Scaricare film protetti da copyright gratuitamente è <b>illegale</b>.</i>
</details>

## 2. Noun Suffixes (Actions and States)

<details>
<summary><b style="color: #569cd6;">-ment</b> (Argue ➔ <b>Argument</b>) - <i>They had a loud <b>argument</b> about politics.</i></summary>
Litigio / Discussione accesa - <i>Hanno avuto un acceso <b>litigio</b> sulla politica.</i>
</details>

<details>
<summary><b style="color: #569cd6;">-ment</b> (Improve ➔ <b>Improvement</b>) - <i>There has been a huge <b>improvement</b> in his grades.</i></summary>
Miglioramento - <i>C'è stato un enorme <b>miglioramento</b> nei suoi voti.</i>
</details>

<details>
<summary><b style="color: #569cd6;">-ion</b> (Discuss ➔ <b>Discussion</b>) - <i>We need to have a serious <b>discussion</b> about our future.</i></summary>
Discussione - <i>Dobbiamo avere una seria <b>discussione</b> sul nostro futuro.</i>
</details>

<details>
<summary><b style="color: #569cd6;">-ion</b> (Inform ➔ <b>Information</b>) - <i>The internet gives us access to limitless <b>information</b>.</i></summary>
Informazione - <i>Internet ci dà accesso a <b>informazioni</b> illimitate.</i>
</details>

<details>
<summary><b style="color: #569cd6;">-ence</b> (Differ ➔ <b>Difference</b>) - <i>Can you tell the <b>difference</b> between butter and margarine?</i></summary>
Differenza - <i>Riesci a notare la <b>differenza</b> tra burro e margarina?</i>
</details>

<details>
<summary><b style="color: #569cd6;">-ence</b> (Depend ➔ <b>Independence</b>) - <i>Moving out of his parents' house gave him <b>independence</b>.</i></summary>
Indipendenza - <i>Trasferirsi fuori da casa dei suoi genitori gli ha dato <b>indipendenza</b>.</i>
</details>

## 3. Noun Suffixes (Concepts and Relationships)

<details>
<summary><b style="color: #569cd6;">-ship</b> (Friend ➔ <b>Friendship</b>) - <i>Their <b>friendship</b> has lasted for over twenty years.</i></summary>
Amicizia - <i>La loro <b>amicizia</b> è durata per oltre vent'anni.</i>
</details>

<details>
<summary><b style="color: #569cd6;">-ship</b> (Leader ➔ <b>Leadership</b>) - <i>The company failed due to poor <b>leadership</b>.</i></summary>
Leadership / Direzione - <i>L'azienda ha fallito a causa di una scarsa <b>leadership</b>.</i>
</details>

<details>
<summary><b style="color: #569cd6;">-dom</b> (Free ➔ <b>Freedom</b>) - <i>The constitution guarantees the <b>freedom</b> of speech.</i></summary>
Libertà - <i>La costituzione garantisce la <b>libertà</b> di parola.</i>
</details>

<details>
<summary><b style="color: #569cd6;">-dom</b> (King ➔ <b>Kingdom</b>) - <i>The lion is considered the ruler of the animal <b>kingdom</b>.</i></summary>
Regno - <i>Il leone è considerato il sovrano del <b>regno</b> animale.</i>
</details>

<details>
<summary><b style="color: #569cd6;">-ness</b> (Happy ➔ <b>Happiness</b>) - <i>Money cannot buy true <b>happiness</b>.</i></summary>
Felicità - <i>I soldi non possono comprare la vera <b>felicità</b>.</i>
</details>

<details>
<summary><b style="color: #569cd6;">-ness</b> (Mad ➔ <b>Madness</b>) - <i>Driving in this heavy snow is absolute <b>madness</b>!</i></summary>
Follia - <i>Guidare con questa neve fitta è pura <b>follia</b>!</i>
</details>

## 4. Adjective Suffixes

<details>
<summary><b style="color: #569cd6;">-ic</b> (Science ➔ <b>Scientific</b>) - <i>We need solid <b>scientific</b> evidence to prove this theory.</i></summary>
Scientifico - <i>Ci servono solide prove <b>scientifiche</b> per dimostrare questa teoria.</i>
</details>

<details>
<summary><b style="color: #569cd6;">-ful</b> (Help ➔ <b>Helpful</b>) - <i>Thank you for the advice, it was very <b>helpful</b>.</i></summary>
Utile / D'aiuto - <i>Grazie per il consiglio, è stato molto <b>utile</b>.</i>
</details>

<details>
<summary><b style="color: #569cd6;">-less</b> (Help ➔ <b>Helpless</b>) - <i>The baby bird fell from the nest and was completely <b>helpless</b>.</i></summary>
Indifeso / Inerme (Senza aiuto) - <i>L'uccellino cadde dal nido ed era completamente <b>indifeso</b>.</i>
</details>

<details>
<summary><b style="color: #569cd6;">-ous</b> (Danger ➔ <b>Dangerous</b>) - <i>It is <b>dangerous</b> to walk alone in that neighborhood at night.</i></summary>
Pericoloso - <i>È <b>pericoloso</b> camminare da soli in quel quartiere di notte.</i>
</details>

<details>
<summary><b style="color: #569cd6;">-al</b> (Nature ➔ <b>Natural</b>) - <i>This product is made with 100% <b>natural</b> ingredients.</i></summary>
Naturale - <i>Questo prodotto è fatto con ingredienti 100% <b>naturali</b>.</i>
</details>
