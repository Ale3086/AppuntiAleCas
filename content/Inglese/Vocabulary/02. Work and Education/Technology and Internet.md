Vocabulary related to computers, hardware, software, and going online.

## 1. Hardware and Devices (A1 - B1)

<details>
<summary><b style="color: #569cd6;">Screen</b> / <b style="color: #569cd6;">Monitor</b> - <i>Staring at a computer <b>screen</b> all day hurts my eyes.</i></summary>
Schermo - <i>Fissare uno <b>schermo</b> del computer tutto il giorno mi fa male agli occhi.</i>
</details>

<details>
<summary><b style="color: #569cd6;">Keyboard</b> / <b style="color: #569cd6;">Mouse</b> - <i>I spilled coffee on my laptop and ruined the <b>keyboard</b>.</i></summary>
Tastiera / Mouse - <i>Ho rovesciato il caffè sul portatile e ho rovinato la <b>tastiera</b>.</i>
</details>

<details>
<summary><b style="color: #569cd6;">Headphones</b> / <b style="color: #569cd6;">Earbuds</b> - <i>I always wear wireless <b>earbuds</b> when I go to the gym.</i></summary>
Cuffie / Auricolari (Earbuds) - <i>Indosso sempre gli <b>auricolari</b> wireless quando vado in palestra.</i>
</details>

<details>
<summary><b style="color: #569cd6;">Flash drive</b> / <b style="color: #569cd6;">Hard drive</b> - <i>My computer crashed because the <b>hard drive</b> was completely full.</i></summary>
Chiavetta USB / Disco fisso - <i>Il mio computer è andato in crash perché il <b>disco fisso</b> era completamente pieno.</i>
</details>

## 2. Using a Computer (A1 - B2)

<details>
<summary><b style="color: #569cd6;">To click on</b> - <i>Don't <b>click on</b> that link, it might be a virus!</i></summary>
Cliccare su - <i>Non <b>cliccare su</b> quel link, potrebbe essere un virus!</i>
</details>

<details>
<summary><b style="color: #569cd6;">To copy</b> / <b style="color: #569cd6;">To cut and paste</b> - <i>Just <b>copy and paste</b> the text into a new document.</i></summary>
Copiare / Tagliare e incollare - <i>Basta <b>copiare e incollare</b> il testo in un nuovo documento.</i>
</details>

<details>
<summary><b style="color: #569cd6;">To log in</b> / <b style="color: #569cd6;">To log out</b> - <i>You need your password to <b>log in</b> to the system.</i></summary>
Accedere / Uscire (da un account) - <i>Ti serve la tua password per <b>accedere</b> al sistema.</i>
</details>

<details>
<summary><b style="color: #569cd6;">Operating system</b> - <i>Which <b>operating system</b> are you using, Windows or Mac?</i></summary>
Sistema operativo - <i>Quale <b>sistema operativo</b> stai usando, Windows o Mac?</i>
</details>

## 3. Going Online (A2 - B2)

<details>
<summary><b style="color: #569cd6;">To go online</b> / <b style="color: #569cd6;">To surf the web</b> - <i>I usually <b>go online</b> to check the news in the morning.</i></summary>
Andare su internet / Navigare in rete - <i>Di solito <b>vado su internet</b> per controllare le notizie al mattino.</i>
</details>

<details>
<summary><b style="color: #569cd6;">Broadband</b> / <b style="color: #569cd6;">Wi-Fi</b> - <i>We just installed high-speed <b>broadband</b> in our house.</i></summary>
Banda larga / Connessione internet veloce - <i>Abbiamo appena installato la <b>banda larga</b> ad alta velocità in casa nostra.</i>
</details>

<details>
<summary><b style="color: #569cd6;">To download</b> / <b style="color: #569cd6;">To upload</b> - <i>It took me ten minutes to <b>upload</b> the video to YouTube.</i></summary>
Scaricare / Caricare (su internet) - <i>Mi ci sono voluti dieci minuti per <b>caricare</b> il video su YouTube.</i>
</details>

<details>
<summary><b style="color: #569cd6;">To stream</b> - <i>I prefer to <b>stream</b> movies rather than downloading them.</i></summary>
Trasmettere in streaming - <i>Preferisco <b>guardare in streaming</b> i film piuttosto che scaricarli.</i>
</details>

## 4. Email Actions

<details>
<summary><b style="color: #569cd6;">To forward</b> - <i>Could you <b>forward</b> that document to the manager?</i></summary>
Inoltrare - <i>Potresti <b>inoltrare</b> quel documento al manager?</i>
</details>

<details>
<summary><b style="color: #569cd6;">To bounce back</b> (C1) - <i>I tried to send the email, but it <b>bounced back</b> because the address was wrong.</i></summary>
Tornare indietro (email non consegnata) - <i>Ho provato a inviare l'email, ma è <b>tornata indietro</b> perché l'indirizzo era sbagliato.</i>
</details>

## 5. Advanced Tech and Software (B2 - C2)

<details>
<summary><b style="color: #569cd6;">To troubleshoot</b> (C1) - <i>The IT guy spent hours trying to <b>troubleshoot</b> the server issue.</i></summary>
Risolvere problemi (tecnici) / Fare diagnostica - <i>Il tizio dell'IT ha passato ore a cercare di <b>risolvere</b> il problema del server.</i>
</details>

<details>
<summary><b style="color: #569cd6;">Glitch</b> / <b style="color: #569cd6;">Bug</b> - <i>There is a minor <b>glitch</b> in the system that deletes emails.</i></summary>
Malfunzionamento / Errore di sistema - <i>C'è un piccolo <b>malfunzionamento</b> nel sistema che cancella le email.</i>
</details>

<details>
<summary><b style="color: #569cd6;">Bandwidth</b> (C1) - <i>We don't have enough <b>bandwidth</b> to support video calls for everyone.</i></summary>
Larghezza di banda (o "capacità/risorse" in gergo aziendale) - <i>Non abbiamo abbastanza <b>larghezza di banda</b> per supportare le videochiamate per tutti.</i>
</details>

<details>
<summary><b style="color: #569cd6;">To back up</b> / <b style="color: #569cd6;">Backup</b> - <i>Always <b>back up</b> your files to the cloud.</i></summary>
Fare il backup / Copia di sicurezza - <i>Fai sempre il <b>backup</b> dei tuoi file sul cloud.</i>
</details>

<details>
<summary><b style="color: #569cd6;">Cutting-edge</b> / <b style="color: #569cd6;">State-of-the-art</b> (C1) - <i>This laboratory uses <b>cutting-edge</b> technology.</i></summary>
All'avanguardia / Di ultima generazione - <i>Questo laboratorio usa una tecnologia <b>all'avanguardia</b>.</i>
</details>

<details>
<summary><b style="color: #569cd6;">Obsolete</b> / <b style="color: #569cd6;">Outdated</b> - <i>Floppy disks are completely <b>obsolete</b> now.</i></summary>
Obsoleto / Superato - <i>I floppy disk sono completamente <b>obsoleti</b> ora.</i>
</details>

<details>
<summary><b style="color: #569cd6;">To hack into</b> / <b style="color: #569cd6;">Breach</b> (C1) - <i>The hackers managed to <b>breach</b> the company's security firewall.</i></summary>
Hackerare / Violare (la sicurezza) - <i>Gli hacker sono riusciti a <b>violare</b> il firewall di sicurezza dell'azienda.</i>
</details>
